# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes good-faith technical disagreement, negative results,
careful criticism, and corrections. Contributions are expected to remain
professional and focused on evidence.

Participants must not harass, threaten, demean, discriminate against, dox, or
deliberately expose private information about other participants. Do not use
issues, pull requests, or discussions to target individuals, manufacture
attribution disputes, or pressure people to accept scientific claims that the
evidence does not support.

When reviewing work:

- criticize claims, code, data, and methods rather than people;
- preserve attribution and upstream credit;
- disclose conflicts or material provenance concerns;
- treat uncertainty and failed experiments as legitimate results;
- do not knowingly publish embargoed Challenge submission material.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these norms,
and may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

For conduct concerns that should not be handled publicly, contact the
maintainer privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile.
